# -Sales-and-Finance-Analytics-Excel-Reports
Excel analytics portfolio demonstrating sales performance analysis, financial reporting, and KPI-driven insights for business stakeholders.

## Project Overview
This project focuses on creating **Sales and Finance analytics reports using Microsoft Excel**.  
The goal is to help businesses **understand performance, track key metrics, and support better decision-making** through clear and meaningful reports.

The project includes customer performance analysis, sales target comparisons, and detailed financial (Profit & Loss) reporting.

---

## Sales Analytics

### Project Objectives
- Create a **customer performance report**
- Compare **market performance against sales targets**

### Purpose of Sales Analytics
- Help businesses **monitor and evaluate sales performance**
- Provide visibility into sales activities across markets and customers

### Importance of Analyzing Sales Data
- Identify sales trends and patterns
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Understand which customers and markets perform best

### Role of Sales Reports
- Help determine effective customer discounts
- Support negotiations with customers
- Identify opportunities for business expansion in high-potential countries

---

## Finance Analytics

### Project Objectives
- Create **Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by Fiscal Year and Month**
- Create **Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by Market**

### Purpose of Finance Analytics
- Evaluate overall financial performance
- Support management decision-making
- Communicate financial results clearly to stakeholders

### Importance of Analyzing Finance Data
- Benchmark performance against previous periods
- Compare results with industry standards
- Provide a strong foundation for budgeting and forecasting

### Role of Finance Reports
- Align financial planning with business strategy
- Improve confidence in the organization’s financial outlook
